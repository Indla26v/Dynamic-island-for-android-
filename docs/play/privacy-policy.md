# Island privacy policy

_Last updated: 24 October 2026_

Island ("the app") shows your notifications and live activities in an island at your phone's camera cutout. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does with it.

## What Island accesses

- **Notifications and media sessions**, through Android's notification access. Island reads them to decide what to show and to let you reply, answer or control music from the island.
- **The list of on-screen windows**, through Android's accessibility service. Island reads window types and positions only, so it can hide during full-screen video, hide when the notification shade is open, and know which app is in front. It never reads the content of any window.
- **Device status**: charging state, battery level, ringer mode, and the names of connected headphones, to show brief status glances.

## What Island does with it

Everything is processed **on your phone**. Island has **no internet permission**, so it cannot send anything anywhere. Nothing is sold, shared or uploaded. Avatars, album art and recent notification text are kept in memory only while they're shown. Your settings and per-app choices are stored in the app's private storage on the device.

## Your choices

You can turn off notification access or the accessibility service at any time in Android settings, and Island stops showing anything. Uninstalling Island deletes all of its data.

## Children

Island is not directed at children.
