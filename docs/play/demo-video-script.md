# Demo video script (about 60 to 90 seconds)

Play reviewers need to see the accessibility overlay drawn over the status bar, and the disclosure shown before the permission. Record the screen on a real phone with the built-in screen recorder.

1. **Fresh install → onboarding (15 s).** Open Island and show the four-step checklist.
2. **Notification access (5 s).** Tap *Allow*, toggle Island on in system settings, and come back. Step 1 ticks.
3. **Prominent disclosure (10 s).** Tap *Allow* on Accessibility. Hold on the disclosure dialog long enough to read it, then tap *Accept*. Turn Island on in Settings → Accessibility and come back. Step 2 ticks.
4. **Finish (3 s).**
5. **Island over the status bar (25 s).** Under *Try it*:
   - *Message*: the island pops at the cutout, over the status bar. Tap it to expand, tap *Reply*, type and send.
   - *Call*: incoming call card, tap the green button, and the compact pill shows a timer.
   - *Music*: go to the home screen; art and bars appear, tap to expand, then pause and skip.
6. **Gets out of the way (10 s).** Open a full-screen video and the island hides. Pull down the shade and it hides too.
7. **Turn off (5 s).** Settings → Accessibility → Island → off. The island disappears.

Voice-over or captions should say that Island never reads screen content and has no internet access.
