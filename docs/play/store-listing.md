# Store listing

**App name:** Island

**Short description (80 chars max):**
> Notifications, calls and music in a smooth island at your camera cutout.

**Full description:**

> Island turns your camera cutout into a live, interactive island.
>
> - **Notifications** pop at the cutout and expand into a card. Reply inline, mark as read, or open the chat. Group chats are read message by message, and repeated media is collapsed ("Arpit + 2 sent 3 reels").
> - **Calls**: answer or decline from the island, with a running timer while you talk.
> - **Music**: album art and live bars. Expand to scrub, pause and skip.
> - **Progress**: downloads, food deliveries and rides fill a ring right at the top.
> - **Quick glances** for charging, low battery, ringer mode and headphones.
> - Two or three things at once? They show as bubbles beside the island.
>
> **Feels like part of the phone.** One physics-based animation, auto-aligned to your cutout, real blur behind the card where your phone supports it. It stays out of your way in full-screen video, landscape and the notification shade.
>
> **Private by design.** Island has no internet permission. Everything stays on your phone.
>
> Setup takes about two minutes. Island needs notification access, and the accessibility service to draw over the status bar. It never reads what's on your screen.

**Category:** Personalization
